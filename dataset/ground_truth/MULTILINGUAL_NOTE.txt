MULTILINGUAL FIXTURES — SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
=============================================

doc04_hindi, doc05_gujarati, doc06_mixed_en_hi and doc07_mixed_en_gu are
SYNTHETIC DEVELOPMENT FIXTURES generated by scripts/make_multilingual_docs.py.
Ground truth is exact by construction: the generator writes the same strings
it renders.

WHAT THEY MEASURE
  Whether the OCR engine + language configuration can read a CLEAN, digitally
  rendered page in each script, and whether script detection routes it to the
  right language pack.

WHAT THEY DO NOT MEASURE
  Real-world accuracy. They contain no scanning noise, no skew from a real
  scanner bed, no paper texture, no ink bleed, no faded stamps, no handwriting,
  and no regional typography variation. A near-zero CER on these fixtures is
  therefore NOT evidence of production readiness for Hindi or Gujarati.

REQUIRED BEFORE ANY ACCURACY CLAIM
  A held-out evaluation set of real (or realistically degraded) Hindi and
  Gujarati land records with human-verified ground truth, kept separate from
  these tuning fixtures. Target 30-50 documents per script per
  05_DATASET_AND_ANNOTATION.
